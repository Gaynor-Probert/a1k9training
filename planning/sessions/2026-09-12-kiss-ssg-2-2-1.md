# Session — 2026-09-12: Upgrade to kiss-ssg 2.2.1 and adopt aliases and {{link}}

## Intent (captured at kiss-branch-open)

**Objective:** Move the site from kiss-ssg 2.1.0 to 2.2.1 and take up the two
2.2.x features it has a use for — `aliases` (so kiss writes `_redirects` and
can report a page removed or moved without one) and `{{link}}` (so an internal
link is resolved by page id at render and a dead one fails the build) — with
the build output otherwise byte-identical.

**Success criteria:**

- [ ] `package.json` asks for `^2.2.1`, the lockfile resolves it, and
      `npx kiss-ssg check --summary generate.js` is `ok` with 19 pages,
      0 failed and no `broken link:` line (baseline at open: 19 pages, 0 failed,
      570 internal references, none broken).
- [ ] `src/assets/_redirects` is deleted. Its 14 rules now live as `aliases`:
      7 on the course records in `src/models/courses/*.json`, 2 on the
      consultation records in `src/models/behavioural-consultations/*.json`,
      4 on the about records in `src/models/about/*.json`, and `/find-us/` on
      the contact `.page()` in `generate.js`.
- [ ] The generated `docs/_redirects` carries exactly those 14 sources, each
      as one `<old> <canonical> 301` line, targets identical to the hand-written
      file's (`/courses/puppy-socialisation`, `/about/`, `/contact/`, …).
      `report().redirects` shows `aliases: 14`, `collisions: []`,
      `removed: []`, `moved: []`. The report's asset count drops from 92 to 91.
- [ ] No hardcoded internal page `href` remains in `src/pages`, `src/layouts`
      or `src/partials`; every one is `{{link "<id>" canonical=true}}` (ids per
      `AIKB/site-map.md`: `index`, `contact`, `courses/index`,
      `courses/course/<slug>`, `behavioural-consultations/index`,
      `behavioural-consultations/consultation/<slug>`, `about/index`,
      `about/<slug>`). Asset hrefs (`/fonts/…`, `/favicon.ico`) stay as they
      are — `{{link}}` is for pages.
- [ ] `canonical=true` is mandatory on every `{{link}}`: this site is not
      `extensionLess`, so a bare `{{link}}` renders `/courses/bronze-obedience.html`,
      which Netlify's pretty-URL rule would 301 back to the slashless form and
      `qa:preview`'s redirect-free check would fail. The check diff proves the
      swap was lossless: after the `{{link}}` step every page is `=` except
      those that carried a slashless section href (`/courses`, `/about`,
      `/behavioural-consultations` — 4 occurrences at open); those are `~` and
      their only change is the trailing slash. Nothing else in any page moves.
- [ ] The URLs `generate.js` builds by hand (`SECTIONS[].url`, `ladderStep`,
      the breadcrumb child `url`) either go through the same id → served-path
      resolution or are left as they are with a comment saying why — decided
      at the first pulse once it is known whether the `link` helper is callable
      from another helper. Either outcome is acceptable; silent drift between
      the two is not.
- [ ] The five notes the record asks for exist and are stamped with the
      subject hash from `report().aikb.subjects`:
      `AIKB/notes/controllers/{about,behavioural-consultations,course,faqmapper}.md`
      and `AIKB/notes/pipeline/tailwind.md`. The check prints no `note …:` line.
- [ ] `CLAUDE.md`'s Deploy paragraph names `^2.2.1` and what the site now
      relies on from it (aliases → `_redirects`, `{{link}}`, the record under
      `AIKB/`). The `_redirects` sentence under Assets and caching is rewritten:
      the file is generated from `aliases`, not kept in `src/assets`.
- [ ] `.claude/settings.json` enables both `kiss-ssg@kiss-ssg` and
      `kiss-memory@kiss-ssg` (already staged in the working tree at open).
- [ ] Eyeball: `npm run qa` passes (page count, no-Bootstrap, SEO, snapshot,
      compare, axe), and `/pr-verify` against the deploy preview reports every
      `_redirects` rule answering 301 to the same target as before.

**Non-goals / out of scope:**

- No `.feed()` — no page on this site carries a date.
- No switch to `extensionLess`, no change to any served URL, no copy or
  design change.
- No controller refactor beyond what a note needs to describe; no change to
  `qa/baseline/content.json` (the snapshot compare should still pass as is,
  because the page text does not change).
- Not fixing the CRLF/LF churn git warns about on every file; it is noise
  inherited from the working copy, not this branch's.

**Impact surface:** build script & config — the version bump and the contact
alias sit in `generate.js`, and the record under `AIKB/` opts the site into
findings every build. It also reaches **templates & partials** (every internal
href), **data & controllers** (aliases on fan-out records in `src/models`; the
four controller notes), and **assets & deploy** (`_redirects` leaves
`src/assets` and becomes build output; the pipeline note). Blast radius is
every page, which is why the byte-identical criterion carries the weight.

**Expected shape:** planned — the destination is known from the 2.2.0
changelog and the page-id table; the one open question (whether `generate.js`'s
hand-built URLs can share the resolver) is bounded and named above.

**Inherited feedback:** none — first session on this site.

**Inherited at open:**

  and the tailwind pipeline step). Nobody has written a note yet; they are

- Uncommitted before the branch was cut and carried onto it: the 2.2.1 bump in

  `.claude/settings.json`, and a line-endings-only touch on `CLAUDE.md`.
- No `check` diff at open: the record was made this session, so the baseline
  is the state at open by construction.
