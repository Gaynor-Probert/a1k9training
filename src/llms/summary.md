A1K9 Behaviour and Training Academy is Gaynor Probert's dog behaviour and training practice near Swansea, South Wales. Gaynor Probert, CFBA Practitioner of the Year 2014, offers behavioural consultations for problem behaviour including dog-on-dog and dog-on-person aggression, and weekend dog training classes from puppy socialisation through Junior, Bronze, Silver, Gold and Platinum obedience to one-to-one training.
